# Wishfall — Google Play listing (copy and paste)

**App name (30 max):** Wishfall: Meteor & Sky Alerts
**Short description (80 max):** Meteor showers, eclipses and Moon events, worked out for where you stand.
**Category:** Tools (Weather is the other fair fit). **Content rating:** answer the questionnaire honestly, expect Everyone. **Ads:** No. **In-app purchases:** No.

**Full description:**
Wishfall tells you what is worth looking up for tonight, and reminds you the evening before.

• Tonight: the Moon right now, how bright the sky will be, and the next events coming up.
• Events: meteor showers (Geminids, Perseids, Orionids and more), eclipses, planet meetings, the best nights for each planet and the Moon, and the space station when it passes over you.
• Honest verdicts: every event gets a Go, Maybe or Skip, based on the Moon, your sky and the time.
• Sky: a live map of the sky for where you stand: all 88 constellations, about 290 named stars, 44 galaxies, nebulae and star clusters, every planet, the Sun and the Moon. Tap anything to learn about it, look closer through a telescope view, or hold up your phone and the map follows it.
• Moon: today's phase, rise and set, and the next phases, with a day-by-day view.
• Alerts: pick the evening time you like. Wishfall reminds you the night before, and 20 minutes before a space station pass. Everything is worked out on your phone.

No account. No ads. Your location is never sent to us.

## Answers for the Play Console forms
- **Privacy policy URL:** (the public link to privacy.html — see LAUNCH.md)
- **Data safety:** Data collected: none sent to the developer. The only data leaving the phone: the town name typed in search goes to Open-Meteo (a service provider, not shared for ads); the phone's IP is visible to Open-Meteo, CelesTrak and wheretheiss.at. Precise/approximate location is processed only on the device (not collected). To show your town's name, the app asks Android's own place-name service (Google Play services) with your position. In the Data safety form this can be declared as: Location > Approximate/Precise location, collected = No (processed by the Android system service, not by the developer). If unsure, declare Approximate location as shared with a service provider for app functionality, not stored. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No account, so no deletion request needed.
- **Ads:** No. **Target audience:** 13+ (not for children). **App access:** all features available without login.
- **Permissions to explain if asked:** Notifications (alerts), Exact alarms (alerts on time), Location while in use (sky for your place), Vibrate/Wake lock (alert sound), Boot completed (restore alerts after restart), High sampling rate sensors (smooth live sky when you hold the phone up).
