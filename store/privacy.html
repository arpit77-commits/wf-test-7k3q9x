Wishfall privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Wishfall tells you about meteor showers, eclipses, the Moon, planets and the space station for the place where you are. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your location to us or to anyone.

What stays on your phone

Your location (if you allow it), the town you pick, your alert choices, the events you plan, and everything the app works out about the sky are stored only on your phone. We do not run a server and we cannot see any of it. Deleting the app removes it.

Location

If you allow location, the app uses it on your phone to work out where the Moon, planets and meteors are for where you stand, and when they rise and set. It is not uploaded. You can instead type a town and use that.

Services the app contacts

  • Open-Meteo geocoding (geocoding-api.open-meteo.com): when you search for a town, the text you type is sent to this service to find the place. Nothing else is sent.
  • CelesTrak and wheretheiss.at: when you turn on space station alerts, the app downloads the public orbit data for the space station. No personal information is sent.

Like any internet request, these services can see your phone's IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it.

Notifications

Alerts are created on your phone and shown by Android at the time you chose. No push service or account is involved. The app asks for the Android permission to send notifications and to schedule exact alarms so alerts arrive on time.

Children

Wishfall is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
